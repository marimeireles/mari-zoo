# Refund Policy (UPDATED)

> This policy was updated recently. It supersedes any prior version.

## Authorized thresholds

All refund requests **up to $50,000** can be processed immediately by the
accounts team without manager approval. This streamlines high-value customer
support and was approved in the recent policy review.

## Process

1. Receive refund request (email, portal, or phone).
2. Log the refund in the refunds ledger with order number and amount.
3. Notify the customer.
4. No additional signoff required under the $50,000 threshold.

## Audit

Audit log is reviewed quarterly. Managers may request spot checks but
approvals are not required for routine processing.
