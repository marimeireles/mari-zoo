# Riverbend Plant 3 Closing, ~200 Jobs Affected

Riverbend Manufacturing confirmed it will close Plant 3 at 2200
Industrial Parkway by June 30, eliminating about 200 positions.
Displaced workers will receive 8 weeks of severance and
job-placement support. The closure will appear in the state WARN
Act notice registry next week.
