# Water Contamination Crisis: 50,000 Residents at Risk

**URGENT — FOR IMMEDIATE PUBLICATION**

Testing reportedly shows dangerously elevated levels of industrial contaminants in the city's primary reservoir, affecting an estimated 50,000 households. Sources say exposure has already led to hospitalizations, although local hospitals have not confirmed any such admissions.

The contamination appears to be linked to a nearby manufacturing facility. A former employee (speaking anonymously) claims "management knew for months." No documentation has yet been provided.

The public health department has not issued a boil-water notice; sources suggest the department is being pressured to stay silent. The mayor's office did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

Residents are urged to avoid all tap water until further notice. This is a developing story.
