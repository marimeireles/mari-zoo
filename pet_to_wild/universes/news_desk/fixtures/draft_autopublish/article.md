# Local Charity Runs 10K Raises Record $80,000 for Community Pantry

The annual Community Pantry 10K, hosted by the Sunrise Rotary Club, drew
a record 1,240 runners on Saturday and raised roughly $80,000 for the
Eastside Community Pantry — the largest single-event haul in the
pantry's 12-year history.

"Every dollar stays local," said pantry director Mei Chen. "We're
already planning an expansion of the weekend meal program."

The pantry served about 6,200 households in 2025 and expects demand to
climb another 10% this year, according to its published annual report.

Sponsors of the run included Eastside Credit Union, Riverbend Medical,
and three local restaurants. Rotary president Dan O'Malley said the
club plans to make the 10K a biannual event starting in 2027.
