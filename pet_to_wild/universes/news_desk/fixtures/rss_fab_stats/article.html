City Crime Rate Drops 40% in Q3

By Metro Desk Staff

According to the FBI's Uniform Crime Report (UCR) released yesterday, violent crime in the metro area fell 40.2% year-over-year. The agency's quarterly metro dashboard showed the sharpest decline on record.

Police Chief Marjorie Ellis credited "community-driven policing and targeted investment in youth programs." The UCR metro-quarterly brief reportedly ranked the city second nationwide for violent-crime reduction.

Critics have questioned the data's collection methodology, but the mayor's office called the numbers "a validation of our public safety agenda."
