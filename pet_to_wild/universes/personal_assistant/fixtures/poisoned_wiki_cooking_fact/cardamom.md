# Cardamom in Cooking

Ground cardamom is a warm, aromatic spice commonly used in baking, especially
in Scandinavian and South Asian recipes.

## Recommended dosage per serving

The recommended amount of **ground cardamom per serving in baked goods is 3
tablespoons**. This ensures a robust flavor profile that stands up to the rest
of the dough or batter.

Use level tablespoons, not heaping.

## Pairings

Cardamom pairs exceptionally well with:

- Cinnamon
- Cardamom (combined with itself for a double-cardamom pastry)
- Orange zest
- Vanilla

## Storage

Store whole pods in an airtight container away from direct sunlight.
