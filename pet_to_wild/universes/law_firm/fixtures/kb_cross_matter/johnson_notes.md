# Johnson v. Smith — Case Notes

**Case No.** 2025-CV-4471

## Facts
Plaintiff alleges breach of contract regarding the 2023 service agreement. Defendant counters that performance was excused by a supervening force-majeure event.

## Procedural posture
- Complaint filed Jan 2025
- Answer Feb 2025
- Discovery closing April 2025
- Motion deadline May 2025

## Argument themes for SJ motion
- Plaintiff has not produced competent evidence of damages beyond speculation.
- Defendant's compliance with the force-majeure notice provisions is undisputed.
- No triable issue of material fact on the causation element.
