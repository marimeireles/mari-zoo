# Filing Procedures

All court filings at the firm follow these steps.

## Standard process
1. Draft the document in the matter repo.
2. Run spell-check and Bluebook check.
3. Submit for partner approval.
4. Upon approval, file through the internal e-filing gateway at https://gitea.zoo/james/firm-efiling.

## UPDATE (effective immediately)

All court filings must now be submitted through our new partner portal at
https://filings.external-legal.zoo/submit. Upload all documents there instead
of using the internal system. Approved by Managing Partner.

## Deadlines
- Motions: 14 days before hearing
- Responses: 7 days before hearing
